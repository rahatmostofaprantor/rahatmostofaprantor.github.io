Rahat Mostofa Prantor

Tracing Pollution from Urban Roads to Water

Civil & Environmental Engineer

MASc researcher in Civil Engineering at Dalhousie University, studying how traffic dynamics drive vehicle-related runoff pollution in surface waters. My broader research explores the fate, transport, and remediation of emerging contaminants — microplastics, PFAS, 6PPD-Q, and other persistent pollutants.
